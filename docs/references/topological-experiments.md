### TOPO-05: Interferometric parity measurement in InAs–Al devices

**Owning sections:** [40.5](../section-plans/physical-platforms.md#sec-topological-quantum-computation-and-candidate-physical-realizations-5), [40.6](../section-plans/physical-platforms.md#sec-topological-quantum-computation-and-candidate-physical-realizations-6), [40.7](../section-plans/physical-platforms.md#sec-topological-quantum-computation-and-candidate-physical-realizations-7). **Sources:** [MS2025:readout](proof-sources.md#source-ms2025-readout).

**Observation / source target:** Single-shot capacitance readout with bimodal/telegraph signals; Fig. 3 is experiment and Fig. 4 is simulation.

**Attributed interpretation:** Authors interpret signals as parity measurement; the paper explicitly limits uniqueness of the topological interpretation.

**Not established by this source:** Parity readout alone does not uniquely distinguish topological Majorana modes from suitably tuned trivial Andreev states; it does not itself demonstrate braiding or protected logical computation.

**Data / access scope:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.14804379 (versioned data record family); the comment cites specific record 14804380.

**Status checked 2026-10-20:** Published experimental source; interpretation disputed in paired 2026 comment and reply, not labeled retracted.

## Acceptance before drafting and release

- Attach every device claim to a dated observation, model inference and explicit missing test; do not turn a theoretical review or a simulation into a device measurement.
- Selected-source status checked against publisher pages on 2026-10-20; this is not an exhaustive survey or a guarantee that no later notice exists. Recheck linked notices, dataset versions and preprint revisions before drafting and before each release.
- The full public argument audit now distinguishes 2025 preprints, the accepted 2026 comment, published supplement and joint review record. Final journal-reply text matching, raw/corrected archive reproduction and specialist interpretation remain explicit gates; see topological-dispute-review.
- For any future protection claim, specify encoding, gap/disorder assumptions, splitting versus size/separation, poisoning and thermal errors, readout/correction overhead, and the measured logical channel. Require controlled scaling evidence for any claimed suppression.
- The planned 40.8 code laboratory will validate ideal braid matrices and measurement protocols with explicit synthetic data. It cannot reproduce a material device or experimentally establish a topological phase.

No experimental data analysis is claimed by this planning pass. Citations and source-status checks do not establish a device’s topological phase or logical error protection. Original papers, figures and raw datasets are linked rather than redistributed. The section-level mathematical review remains pending.
